## 🌍 What we do

Flora Carbon AI is a climate-tech company building **high-quality, nature-based carbon projects** and the **digital MRV infrastructure** to verify them. We work with project developers and smallholder farmers to develop afforestation, reforestation, agroforestry, REDD+, improved forest management, and blue carbon projects — and pair them with AI tooling that turns field evidence into **registry-ready, audit-defensible reporting**.

Our goal is simple: bring **transparency, traceability, and credibility** to the voluntary carbon market, and route carbon finance directly to the communities restoring nature on the ground.

## 🔄 End-to-end MRV platform

Our platform supports the full project lifecycle across **50+ methodologies** — spanning agroforestry, A/R, REDD+, IFM, and blue carbon.

| Phase | Focus |
|-------|-------|
| **01 · Pre-feasibility & screening** | Project-type fit, land eligibility, desktop assessment, carbon & financial scenario modelling, and risk screening. |
| **02 · Project development** | Methodology screening, boundary & GIS support, stakeholder planning, PDD drafting, and monitoring framework design. |
| **03 · Monitoring, reporting & audit prep** | Implementation planning, evidence packs, monitoring reports, verification-readiness controls, and audit support. |
| **04 · Credit commercialisation** | Traceability framework, submission-ready outputs, issuance planning, and buyer/investor documentation. |
